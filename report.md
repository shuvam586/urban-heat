# Completed Research — Structured Synthesis Report

## 1. Repository & Experimental Architecture

**Design.** The implemented study is the simplified, script-based pipeline in `PLAN.md` (not the broader notebook/gated design in `readme.md`). One parameterised recipe is applied independently to five cities — Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad — with no pooling. Each script takes `--city`, reads only `config.py`, writes only its own path. Execution protocol: Hyderabad first, then the other four; every step must pass its `check_XX` before proceeding; every result is delivered with "Done / Shows / Could be wrong" sentences.

**Spatial-temporal frame.** Fixed 50 km radius about each city centre; a 334×334 grid of **300 m EPSG:7755** (India NSF LCC) cells (~111,556; ~87,000 retained inside the circle), with deterministic `grid_id = "{col}_{row}"`. Years **2022, 2023, 2025**, season **1 March–1 June** (March–May composite). Unit = 300 m cell × year.

**Data sources.** GHSL SMOD P2023A v2.0 (2020 epoch) for the rural→centre ladder (rural 11–13, suburban 21, semi-dense 22, dense 23, centre 30; water 10 excluded); GHS-BUILT-S 2020 built fraction (`built_surface/10000`, 100 m→300 m mean); Landsat 8/9 C2 L2 for LST/NDVI (and NDBI later); JRC Global Surface Water occurrence >50% for the water screen. RQ2 Part C adds Dynamic World (built probability), ESA WorldCover v200 (class 50), and Landsat NDBI.

**Scale factors / masks.** LST = `ST_B10 × 0.00341802 + 149.0` K; SR = `SR_B × 0.0000275 − 0.2`; NDVI = `(SR_B5−SR_B4)/(SR_B5+SR_B4)`. Cloud mask = QA_PIXEL bits 1–5 (dilated cloud, cirrus, cloud, cloud shadow, snow) plus `ST_QA×0.01 ≤ ST_QA_MAX_K`. **QC thresholds:** `ST_QA_MAX_K = 5 K` (post-hoc, from the Chennai 3 K diagnostic), `MIN_CLEAR_OBS = 3`, `MAX_MISSING_LST_SHARE = 0.10`, `MIN_RURAL_CELLS = 200`. Outcome `lst_rel` = cell LST minus the median LST of rural (SMOD 11–13) cells in that city-year.

**Uncertainty design.** All inference uses **5 km spatial blocks** (`block_id = floor(x/5000)_floor(y/5000)` on projected metres, fixed across years). Step 04 uses a 1,000-draw block bootstrap (2.5/97.5 percentiles, seed 42) for class-median intervals; GAMs use `pygam.LinearGAM(s(built)+s(ndvi)+te(built,ndvi)+f(year))` on a 10,000-cell random subsample (seed 42, all years of sampled cells), smoothing by `gridsearch` over λ∈{0.1,1,10,100,1000}, scored with **5-fold GroupKFold** on `block_id`. Vegetation rule v3.1 uses city-wide NDVI p25/p75 fixed contrast with a 200-draw block-bootstrap CI and a 5% coverage gate (`VEG_MIN_RANGE_SHARE`).

**Verification tests (check_01–09).** `check_01` grid schema/unique ids/valid classes/built∈[0,1]/~5% cell-count tolerance; `check_02` Landsat schema/years/LST∈[270,340] K/missing≤0.10; `check_03` panel schema/no water/≥200 rural cells/rural median lst_rel=0; `check_04` gradient schema/all 5 classes/CI ordering/cell counts vs panel; `check_05` cv.json keys/convergence/model-comparison fold equality/Fig 3 transposed mask correctness/sparse counts; `check_06` summary phrases, class-23 and sparse counts, curve direction consistent with cv.json; `check_07` RQ1 CSV with ≥200 cells and ≥30 blocks per reference, Ref_A medians matching gradient.csv within 1e-6 K; `check_08` RQ2 context fold alignment with cv.json, eligibility thresholds (≥500 cells, ≥30 blocks), no extrapolation beyond built q01–q099, Part B fold-difference consistency; `check_09` RQ2 metrics fold alignment, metrics covering panel grid/year keys, DW/WC ∈[0,1], **strict NDBI ∈[−1,1]**, per-metric CV consistency. All nine passed for all five cities.

## 3. Research Questions & Hypotheses Answered (and Scope Cuts)

**RQ1 — Gradient (answered).** Class-wise `lst_rel` contrasts with block-bootstrap intervals under three reference definitions. **H1:** supported with the stated caveat — the centre is *not* uniformly hotter: Hyderabad (−2.22/−1.68/−1.98 K) and Bengaluru (−1.04/−1.00/−0.94 K) have cooler-than-rural centres in all years, while Delhi (+1.01 K in 2025) and Kolkata are warmer. Magnitude and even sign depend on the reference (H1's "may be small or negative by day where rural land is bare").

**RQ2 — Response and context (partially answered).**
- **H2 nonlinearity:** descriptive fitted total effects are non-monotonic (Delhi), falling (Hyderabad, Bengaluru), rising (Kolkata), or roughly flat (Chennai), but the *formal* linear-vs-nonlinear CV test shows built-up nonlinearity adds **negligible** out-of-sample skill everywhere (|mean gain| ≤ 0.0023 R², all < 0.005); high-built bins (>0.4, 3–7% of cells) are unsupported.
- **H3 / vegetation moderation (via v3.1):** **untestable with this data** in all five cities — bin-20 fixed-NDVI coverage 0.0–3.3% < 5%, so all contrasts are extrapolation and reported as exploratory only. Large positive contrasts (+0.19 to +5.27 K) exist but are not supported tests.
- **H4 city differences:** clearly confirmed (centre medians, curve directions, CV R² all differ across cities).
- **H5 built-up metric:** **supported** — NDBI conflates dry bare soil with built-up land (see §4).
- **H6 context dependence:** **supported** — at the same built fraction, urban contexts differ substantially (e.g., Delhi built 0.15: centre +0.142 K vs semi-dense +2.250 K; Hyderabad: centre −2.479 K vs suburban −1.499 K; Bengaluru: centre −1.320 K vs suburban −0.439 K).

**RQ3 — Trajectory / H7 (excluded).** Not implemented. `PLAN.md` explicitly places "built-up history and trajectory" under *Deliberately left out*; no GHSL history epochs, nested history models, or matched comparisons were run. H7 (built-up age adds information beyond current fraction) is therefore **unanswered by design**, not refuted.

**Scope note.** `readme.md` describes a much broader aspirational design (RQ3; datasets GHS-POP, GHS-BUILT-H, SRTM, ERA5-Land, CHIRPS, MODIS night LST; leave-one-year-out/leave-one-city-out validation; notebooks, gates, manifests). The completed work follows the simplified `PLAN.md` only; no population, height, climate, night-LST, threshold, leave-one-out, or longitudinal analysis exists.

## 4. Core Empirical Conclusions & Key Numbers

**Centre-class median `lst_rel` (Ref_A, K), 2022/2023/2025:** Delhi −0.30/+0.14/+1.01; Hyderabad −2.22/−1.68/−1.98; Kolkata +0.38/+0.20/+0.41; Chennai −0.06/+0.54/+0.04; Bengaluru −1.04/−1.00/−0.94. Span −2.22 K (Hyderabad 2022) to +1.01 K (Delhi 2025); Hyderabad and Bengaluru are cooler than rural throughout.

**Reference sensitivity.** Ref_C is cooler than Ref_A in every city-year (Ref_C−Ref_A median LST from −0.561 K Kolkata 2023 to −2.176 K Bengaluru 2025); Ref_B−Ref_A ranges −0.604 to +0.145 K. Since `lst_rel = cell_LST − ref_LST`, subtracting the cooler Ref_C shifts all urban medians upward. Consequently the centre **sign flips under Ref_C**: Hyderabad 2023 (−1.68→+0.14), Chennai 2022 (−0.06→+1.91), Bengaluru all three years (−1.04/−1.00/−0.94→+0.69/+0.33/+1.23); under Ref_B only Delhi 2022 (−0.30→+0.22); Kolkata never flips. All groups exceed 200 cells and 30 blocks (min Kolkata Ref_B 2025: 3,504/165).

**Baseline GAM CV R² (NDVI+year / built+year / full):** Delhi 0.305/0.011/0.356; Hyderabad 0.546/0.007/0.637; Kolkata 0.332/0.230/0.461; Chennai 0.430/0.032/0.461; Bengaluru 0.461/−0.003/0.553. NDVI+year carries most skill; built+year has independent skill only in Kolkata. Full-model gain over NDVI+year: +0.031 to +0.129 R².

**H6 within-context divergences** (above) and **H2 gains** (Delhi −0.002272 3/5; Hyderabad +0.000483 4/5; Kolkata +0.000183 4/5; Chennai −0.000014 3/5; Bengaluru +0.000181 4/5); derivative zero crossings Delhi 10, Hyderabad 4, Kolkata 1, Chennai 4, Bengaluru 2.

**H5 metric comparison.** NDBI vs `lst_rel`: Hyderabad −0.351, Kolkata +0.654, Chennai +0.786, Delhi +0.583, Bengaluru +0.716 — strongest of the four metrics in every city. NDBI vs built fraction is weak (−0.167 to +0.298) but vs bare-soil proxy is strong (0.56–0.60), while Dynamic World/WorldCover bare-soil correlations are only 0.06–0.34. CV R²: NDBI alone (metric+year) reaches 0.395–0.656, versus GHS-BUILT-S 0.007–0.230, Dynamic World 0.003–0.185, WorldCover −0.009–0.174; with NDVI+interaction all four converge to ≈0.33–0.71. NDBI's small full-model gain (+0.04–0.23) reflects signal already captured linearly (bare soil/dryness).

**Threshold sensitivity.** Raising ST_QA_MAX_K 3→5 K sharply increased retention (Chennai 2022 8.39%→89.33%) while moving Hyderabad/Kolkata centre medians and full CV R² only modestly; 5 K was adopted for all cities.

## 5. Methodological Limitations & Claims Boundaries

- **Associations only, not causal.** Every output is explicitly an association; no causal language is used. The cross-city tables are read descriptively — no pooled models, leave-one-city-out, or hierarchical models.
- **LST ≠ air temperature.** The outcome is satellite **land surface (skin) temperature** at ~10:30 AM Landsat overpass (mid-morning, not afternoon peak). Heatwave/air-temperature claims are out of scope.
- **Post-hoc conventions.** `ST_QA_MAX_K = 5`, `MIN_CLEAR_OBS = 3`, `MAX_MISSING_LST_SHARE = 0.10`, vegetation rules v2→v3.1, the Ref_C definition, the bare-soil proxy, the curve "direction" rule, the 3 K/5 K diagnostic, and the NDBI SR>0 mask were all chosen after seeing results; they are exploratory conventions, not pre-registered or Landsat specifications. All five v3.1 vegetation tests are "not testable with this data."
- **Resolution/measurement limits.** GHSL classes are 1 km while the grid is 300 m (~11 cells per class), so class is context, not a precise per-cell measurement. SMOD and GHS-BUILT-S are related products, so by-class and pooled results overlap. Built fraction is zero-inflated (>87% below 0.1 in some cities); curves are unsupported above built ≈0.4 (3–7% of cells).
- **Modelling caveats.** GAMs fit a 10,000-cell subsample; the 95% band is conditional on the selected smoothing; model-based SE ignores spatial autocorrelation (bootstraps correct for it); LST and NDVI share Landsat scenes/atmosphere/cloud effects and built and NDVI are negatively correlated, so the interaction term is interpreted cautiously. H2's descriptive curve-shape rule is not a formal test.
- **Physical interpretations are hypotheses, not proved mechanisms.** Cooler centre classes in Hyderabad/Bengaluru are consistent with ~900 m elevation (Bengaluru) or land cover but are explicitly untested; the H5 bare-soil reading of NDBI is an interpretation of correlations, not an identified mechanism. Bengaluru had an EE extraction timeout (resolved by tiling) that briefly limited its Part C run.
- **Scope exclusions.** RQ3/H7 trajectory, thresholds, alternative rural references beyond Ref_A/B/C, and all `readme.md`-level datasets/validation schemes were deliberately not implemented; null and untestable results are reported plainly rather than resolved.
